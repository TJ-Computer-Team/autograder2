Welcome to TJCT Grader

This site hosts TJ Computer Team practice problems, in-house contests, submissions, standings, and training material. Use contests for active in-house rounds, use problem links from contests for statements and problem-specific submissions, and check standings after a contest opens them.

This website is intended for TJ students only.

Read the rules before competing

In-house contests follow the TJHSST honor code and grader-specific rules about original work, outside sources, and contest discussion.

About TJ Computer Team

TJ Intermediate Computer Team is a competitive programming club for learning, competing, and practicing with a community of programmers. ICT eighth periods include lectures, activities, and contests such as ACSL.

The club points students toward contests like Codeforces and USACO, and uses in-house competitions on this grader, along with other heuristics, when selecting students for travel competitions.

Source: TJ ICT website.
